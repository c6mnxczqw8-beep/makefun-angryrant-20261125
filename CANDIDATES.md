# Rant candidates (draft pass)

Short rants about everyday annoyances, at most 400 characters,
scored on how hard the fury lands and how funny it stays.

1. The quick update: "Nothing humbles you like a 'quick update'
   that takes 45 minutes and restarts twice." -- universally
   lived; the draft stops early and needs its escalation: the
   moved button, the nothing changed, the rating request.
2. The printer: "The printer knows. It can smell the deadline.
   It has one job and a tray full of malice." -- strong voice,
   short for a rant.
3. The meeting invite: "'Quick call?' is the most violent
   sentence in the language. There is no call. There is a
   hostage situation with a calendar link." -- funny, narrower
   audience.
4. The password rules: "Your password must contain a number, a
   symbol, and the blood of a unicorn, and it expires while you
   are typing it." -- classic shape, well worn.
5. The group chat: "214 unread messages and every single one of
   them is 'lol'." -- true, ends flat.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 1, after a refinement pass. The draft was
only the arrest; the shipped rant is the full trial -- the
moved button, the nothing-else-changed, and the rating request,
which is the true obscenity. The closing surrender ("Five
stars. It knows where I live.") is where exasperation turns
into comedy: the ranter is furious and completely powerless,
which is the everyday annoyance exactly.
